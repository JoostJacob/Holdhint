Install Holdhint
================

1. Drag Holdhint onto the Applications folder.
2. Eject this disk image. Open Holdhint from Applications.
3. macOS shows a dialog with two buttons: Done and Move to Trash.
   Click Done.
   Do not click Move to Trash.
4. Open the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
   Scroll down to Security.
5. Click Open Anyway next to the Holdhint message.
6. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
7. Click Open Anyway again.

Holdhint then opens a welcome window for Input Monitoring. Turn Holdhint
on. The app restarts itself. It has no Dock icon: look for ⌘ in the
menu bar.

Open Anyway stays for about an hour. If the button is gone, open
Holdhint again, click Done, and return to Privacy & Security.

On macOS 14, Control-click Holdhint, choose Open, and confirm Open.
